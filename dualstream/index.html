What is DualStream: Web & USB Cam?

DualStream: Web & USB Cam is a professional mobile and tablet broadcasting application designed for content creators, event organizers, and sports streamers. The app's primary purpose is to allow users to capture high-definition video feeds from internal device cameras, external USB UVC webcams, or HDMI capture cards, overlay interactive web graphics (such as live scoreboards), and securely stream the output directly to platforms like YouTube Live and Facebook Live using authorized API integrations.

Key Features

📹

USB & Built-in Camera Support

Connect external USB UVC webcams or HDMI capture cards, or switch instantly between Front and Back phone/tablet cameras.

🌐

Interactive Web Overlay

Float live web scoreboards, widgets, or web pages over your video stream. Position and resize freely with drag-and-drop handles.

🔴

YouTube & Facebook Live

One-tap account authentication to broadcast high-definition RTMP video streams directly to your YouTube channel or Facebook Page.

🔍

Smart Browser Address Bar

Search Google or enter web addresses directly into the overlay header bar to load sports scorekeepers and graphics on the fly.

🔎

Pinch Zoom & Digital Pan

Pinch-to-zoom up to 5x digital magnification and drag to pan across your camera feed for optimal video framing.

📱

Tablet & Landscape Optimized

Designed for tablets and smartphones in landscape orientation with smooth camera rotation and watchdog stability.
