This Privacy Policy explains how DualStream collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and our Facebook Live integration features.

1. Information We Collect

When you use DualStream, we may collect the following information:

  • Facebook Account Information: If you sign in using Facebook Login, we access basic profile information (such as your public profile and email address) provided by Meta/Facebook OAuth.
  • Live Video Permissions: When you initiate a live stream to Facebook, the app uses Meta Graph API permissions (such as publish_video) solely to create and broadcast live video streams to your Facebook account.
  • Local Application Preferences: Your selected scoresheet web URLs, custom stream thumbnails, and camera layout preferences are stored locally on your device in SharedPreferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to provide and improve app functionality:

  • To authenticate your identity and authorize live video streaming to your designated Facebook Profile or Page.
  • To generate RTMPS stream URLs and upload your chosen stream thumbnails for your live broadcasts.
  • No personal data or Facebook account credentials are sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

We take your privacy seriously:

  • We do not store your Facebook password or personal data on external servers operated by us.
  • Authentication tokens provided by Facebook are stored securely in local device memory and used exclusively for active API sessions.

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

According to Facebook/Meta Platform rules, users have the right to request deletion of their data associated with our app:

If you wish to remove DualStream access and delete associated data from your Facebook account, follow these steps:

  1. Log in to your Facebook account.
  2. Go to Settings & Privacy ➔ Settings ➔ Apps and Websites.
  3. Locate DualStream in the active apps list.
  4. Click Remove to revoke all app permissions and remove associated data.
