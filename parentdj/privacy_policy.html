ParentDJ Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 30, 2026

Soaring Eagle Software ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ParentDJ mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect user data when you use the ParentDJ app.

1. How We Use Google User Data

ParentDJ accesses Google and YouTube user data through official Google APIs (specifically the youtube.readonly scope) solely to deliver core application functionality:

  • To display your personal YouTube Music playlists as playback button cards on your DJ soundboard grid.
  • To retrieve track titles, artist names, and thumbnail artwork for songs inside your selected playlists.
  • To enable seamless, user-controlled audio playback of your YouTube Music tracks during sports games and events.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or tracking purposes.

2. Sharing, Transfer, and Disclosure of Google User Data

Strict Non-Disclosure Guarantee: ParentDJ does NOT sell, trade, rent, transfer, or disclose Google user data or YouTube account information to any third parties, advertisers, data brokers, or external partners.

Google user data is used exclusively within the ParentDJ app interface on your local device to render your music soundboard.

3. Data Protection & Security Mechanisms

We implement robust, industry-standard security mechanisms to protect sensitive user data:

  • Encrypted Device Storage: OAuth authentication tokens are stored locally on your device using hardware-backed OS encryption (Android KeyStore and iOS Keychain).
  • Encrypted Transport: All communication between ParentDJ and Google/YouTube API servers is transmitted securely via HTTPS using TLS 1.2/1.3 encryption.
  • No External Server Storage: Your Google OAuth credentials and YouTube account data are never uploaded to, transmitted through, or stored on any external servers operated by us.

4. Retention and Deletion of Google User Data

ParentDJ retains Google user data only for as long as you choose to keep your YouTube account connected to the app:

  • In-App Disconnection & Local Deletion: You can disconnect your YouTube account at any time by tapping Log Out in ParentDJ’s side drawer menu. Tapping Log Out instantly and permanently deletes all Google OAuth tokens from your device’s local encrypted storage.
  • Google Account Access Revocation: You can revoke ParentDJ’s access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Permissions Page. Upon revocation, ParentDJ immediately loses all access to your YouTube data.

5. Compliance with Google API Services User Data Policy

ParentDJ's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
