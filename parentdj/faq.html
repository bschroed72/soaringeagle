Frequently Asked Questions

What is free in the beta?

During our open beta test, everyone receives free lifetime access on the installed device to all core features, soundboards, and audio tools with no credit card required.

What does $9.99 unlock?

Once publicly launched, the trial version allows 3 Folder cards and 1 Streaming card, 5 custom start times, and Custom Button Colors, as well as full box-sync functionality and unlimited songs per card. An optional one-time $9.99 upgrade unlocks unlimited streaming tracks, unlimited folder cards, unlimited custom start times, excluding songs from random play, Live PA Microphone announcements, and access on multiple devices of the same type (iOS or Android).

Will I need to pay twice for iOS and Android?

Unfortunately, yes. Each platform requires a separate subscription for full access as their app stores are not connected. However, you can use the same box-synced settings across both types of devices.

How does TestFlight / Play beta install work?

For iOS/iPadOS, first install the TestFlight app and then install securely via Apple TestFlight. For Android, join our open beta directly through the Google Play Store beta testing link.

Which streaming services are supported?

ParentDJ supports Apple Music integration for streaming tracks and playlists right alongside your local MP3 files and sound effects.

How does Box sync move setups between devices?

Box Cloud Sync automatically synchronizes your music folder structures, MP3 files, card colors, and settings seamlessly between your iPad, Android tablet, and phone.

How can I contact support?

Reach out to our support team anytime by emailing support@soaringeagle.app.
